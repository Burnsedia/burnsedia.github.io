# Variant: Content-First Cards

## Design stance
A clean, magazine-style content site: the build log is the hero of the page, presented as readable cards with clear hierarchy. Terminal vibes reduced to accents (mono labels, status bar) so content does the work.

## Key choices
- Layout: centered hero → live-build status bar → stat band → blog card grid → courses → newsletter CTA
- Typography: system sans for body/headlines (fast, native), mono reserved for labels, dates, and the status bar
- Color: same darksynthwave palette, but pink used more sparingly (tags, links, numbers)
- Interaction: newsletter form with inline success state; hover lift on cards; sticky header with Subscribe button

## Trade-offs
- Strong at: scannability, readability, content-forward conversion; friendlier to non-dev visitors
- Weak at: less "wow" than the terminal variant; the status bar + stats do the building-in-public work but with less theater

## Best for
The content business pivot with an audience that includes potential clients and subscribers, not just devs. The most balanced variant — closest to "blog first, storefront second."
