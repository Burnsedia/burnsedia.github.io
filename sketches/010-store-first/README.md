# Variant: Store-first Homepage

## Design stance
The store IS the hero. Products and services hit first, the build log becomes the "receipts" section below. Same hybrid language (hexagon in nav, rounded cards, Inter body, mono accents), but the emphasis flips from content-first to sell-first.

## Key choices
- Hero = "I build shit that works — and I sell it here" + full product grid + filter
- Compact hexagon mark in the nav brand, not a big hero hexagon
- Build log demoted to a proof section under the store
- Services + membership follow; newsletter closes

## Trade-offs
- Strong at: converting visitors who arrive to buy; product discovery is instant
- Weak at: less "build in public" identity up top; content takes a back seat

## Best for
If product/service sales are the priority and content is the trust layer beneath them.
